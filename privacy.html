Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the DacaBilling desktop application ("DacaBilling", "we", "us") handles information. DacaBilling is published by Shenal Nethsara.

Information you store in DacaBilling

DacaBilling stores the business records you enter, such as customers, invoices, quotations, receipts, payments, expenses, inventory, logos and attachments. Where this data is kept depends on the work mode you choose:

  • Offline mode: data is stored only on your computer, in your Documents folder. We do not receive it.
  • Online mode with Google (Google Sheets): data is stored in a Google Sheet and a Google Drive folder in your own Google account. It is sent directly between your computer and Google. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this data.
  • Online Workspace (seller-managed): if your seller provides an Online Workspace account, your billing data is stored in a hosted database operated for that service. Access is limited to the members of your workspace and the service administrators.

Google user data

When you choose Sign in with Google, DacaBilling requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. This lets DacaBilling see, create and edit only the Google Drive files and Google Sheets that DacaBilling creates or that you explicitly choose with the Google file picker. DacaBilling cannot see your other Drive files.

  • How we use it: only to create, read and update your DacaBilling Sheet and its attachment folder so the app can show and save your billing records.
  • Storage: your Google sign-in token is stored on your computer, protected with your operating system's secure storage where available. It is not sent to us.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI or machine learning models.
  • Limited Use: DacaBilling's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can disconnect Google from DacaBilling's settings at any time, or remove DacaBilling's access in your Google Account under Third-party apps & services. Data in your Google Sheet and Drive folder stays in your account until you delete it. Offline data can be deleted by removing the DacaBilling folder in your Documents. For Online Workspace data, contact us to request deletion.

Security

Connections to Google use HTTPS and OAuth with PKCE. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please protect your computer and Google account.

Children

DacaBilling is a business tool and is not intended for children under 13.
